# 1. Record decisions

Date: 2026-01-18

## Context

Most of this repo is configuration. Configuration shows what was chosen, but
not what was rejected or why, and that is the part people argue about later.

## Decision

Decisions that shape more than one directory get a short record here: the
context, the choice, and what it costs. Records are not edited after the fact.
A changed mind is a new record that supersedes the old one.

## Consequences

Some decisions will look obvious in hindsight and the record will feel like
noise. That is cheaper than rediscovering a rejected option six months later.
